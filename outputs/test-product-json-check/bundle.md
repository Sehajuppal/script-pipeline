Voice spec: cast a conversational human read — like a real person talking to a friend, never an ad read. Female voice (default casting — swap as needed), warm and confident, never pressuring. Vary pace naturally: unhurried on trust beats, brisk 160–180 wpm on the CTA. Light natural imperfections welcome (breath, pace shifts, emphasis). Never monotone, never a polished commercial delivery. No scripted laughs/sighs.

## STYLE
Human UGC demo, beauty niche. 9:16 vertical. bathroom vanity with a large mirror. soft natural window light from the left. Imperfection welcome (flaws in the BEFORE, faint handheld in candid beats) — polish kills trust in this format. Transitions: hard cuts by default; match-cut on transformation beats. On-screen graphic text (labels like BEFORE/AFTER, TEST 1): bold sans, high contrast, max 3 words per card. Safe zones: keep graphic text and key action clear of the top 250px, bottom 320px (IG UI overlays), and the right rail. Product accuracy: match the PRODUCT block above exactly — no invented branding. Audio: voiceover clean and forward; neutral upbeat background music bed low underneath, ducked under speech.
- Maximum-contrast before/after is the genre's currency — make the transformation unmistakable.
- Motion toward the camera reads as confidence; stillness reads as ad.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT — never render
Warped faces, extra fingers or limbs, melted hands, garbled / misspelled / merged text, watermarks or logos, burned-in captions or subtitles, letterbox or pillarbox bars, plastic CGI skin, stock-photo stiffness. If any on-screen text cannot be rendered cleanly, drop the text and keep the shot — never ship garbled text.
