## STYLE
Human UGC pov-vlog, food niche. 9:16 vertical. a clean modern home interior. soft natural daylight. Imperfection welcome (flaws in the BEFORE, faint handheld in candid beats) — polish kills trust in this format. Transitions: hard cuts by default; match-cut on transformation beats. On-screen graphic text (labels like BEFORE/AFTER, TEST 1): bold sans, high contrast, max 3 words per card. Safe zones: keep graphic text and key action clear of the top 250px, bottom 320px (IG UI overlays), and the right rail. Product accuracy: match the PRODUCT block above exactly — no invented branding. Audio: voiceover clean and forward; neutral upbeat background music bed low underneath, ducked under speech.
- Fast cold open, proof before pitch — the first frame must work with sound off.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT — never render
Warped faces, extra fingers or limbs, melted hands, garbled / misspelled / merged text, watermarks or logos, burned-in captions or subtitles, letterbox or pillarbox bars, plastic CGI skin, stock-photo stiffness. If any on-screen text cannot be rendered cleanly, drop the text and keep the shot — never ship garbled text.
