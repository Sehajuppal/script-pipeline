# Short-form virality: quantified, encodable evidence (cut-off 2026-09-28)

Scope: TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts scripts for cheap e-commerce products, with a human UGC or AI-persona presenter.
Evidence tags: **[P]** binding platform or legal rule · **[S]** strong (first-party platform research, peer-reviewed work, large controlled tests) · **[M]** moderate (large third-party or vendor datasets, single field studies) · **[A]** anecdotal or directional. Figures from ads are marked as such.

---

## 1. Hooks (0–3 s)

- **Proposition within 3 s, hook finished by 6 s.** TikTok's ad guidance says to state the proposition in the first 3 s and land the hook within 6 s. It also asks for 9:16, at least 720p, safe-zone text, visible people and a lo-fi look ([TikTok Ads Help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/creative-best-practices)). About 90% of ad-recall impact happens in the first 6 s ([Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads)). TikTok counts a paid "focused view" as 6 s watched or an interaction within 6 s ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/focused-view-optimization-updates-to-video-view-objective)). [S/P]
- **Product in the first frames.**
  - Ads showing the product on screen got +65% brand affinity and +25% recall (Creative Codes). [S]
  - TikTok Shop Academy (20 May 2026): the hook is the first 3–6 s and must be about the featured product ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-shoppable-video-content-guide-tells-creators-what-actually-works/)). [P]
  - In OpusClip's 34,635-clip TikTok sample, product or outcome "showcase" hooks averaged about 6,037 views. The most common type, "shock/surprise", averaged about 1,973 ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/anatomy-of-a-viral-tiktok-2026)). [M]
- **A person, text and sound.** Across 1.2M Meta Reels ads:
  - a visible human gave +27% CTR;
  - text stickers gave +11 points on positive response;
  - music plus voice-over gave +15 points.

  Adding creative with 9:16, audio, safe-zone placement and at least one native element (such as an early hook) to existing campaigns improved CPA by 16%, ROAS by 13% and conversion rate by 29% ([Meta Reels playbook](https://irp.cdn-website.com/e41755d3/files/uploaded/Reels+Playbook+from+Meta.pdf)). The same creative saw +30% 3-second play rate and +56% CTR ([Adobe citing Meta](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2024/11/04/unlock-potential-reels-best-practices-for-creative-success-using-adobe-creative-cloud)). [S]
- **The window is about 1–3 s.** Creator Jenny Hoyos says Shorts give about 1 s to hook; her formula is shock → intrigue → satisfy ([YouTube blog](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-shorts-deep-dive/)). [A]
- **Ad hook-rate benchmarks (3-second plays ÷ impressions).** 2026 medians are 28% on Meta, 33% on TikTok and 22% on YouTube. The top 10% reach 45%, 55% and 38% (Benly, 12k+ ads). Under 15% on Meta is usually a signal to kill the ad (Ryze AI; both via [AdBeacon](https://www.adbeacon.com/2026-creative-benchmarks-are-out/)). [M]
- **Tactics that produce winners (Meta).** Motion analysed 578,750 creatives; about 5% became winners. Newness, Sale Announcement, Price Anchor and Urgency hooks had the highest winner rates, at 6–11%. Confession, Contrarian and Warning hooks are higher-variance. The data includes Black Friday ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-hook-tactics)). Winner rate by format: Unboxing 9.8%, Offer-first banner 8.6%, Demo 8.1%, Testimonial 6.5% ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/top-visual-formats)). [M]
- **Word choice (from headline A/B tests; applying it to spoken hooks is an inference).**
  - Each negative word raised CTR by 2.3% (105k variants, 370M impressions; [Robertson 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-023-01538-4)).
  - "I" and third-person pronouns helped, "we" hurt, and "you" and readability had no significant effect ([Gligorić 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10038272/)).
  - For video, about 8 words per sentence was optimal ([Kim 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10660-024-09926-2)).

  [S; M]
- **Folklore; don't encode as fact.** "63% of top ads land the message in 3 s" (ads only), "71% decide in 3 s", "65% 3-second hold → 4–7× impressions" and "0.4 s decision" have no traceable primary source ([source audit](https://tugan.ai/blog/how-to-write-tiktok-hooks)). [A]

## 2. Retention, length, pace, text, safe zones

- **What TikTok's ranker rewards (official).** Finishing a longer video is a strong signal. Likes, shares, comments and replays are the core interaction signals. Follower count is not a direct factor. Duplicated and spam content is ineligible ([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-my/how-tik-tok-recommends-video-for-you)). [P/S]
- **Curve shape: a steep drop over 0–5 s, then gradual decay.** [S/M]
  - SnapUGC (90k Snapchat videos): watch time per video is bimodal, meaning videos tend to be either skipped quickly or watched substantially. The share of viewers watching past 5 s doesn't depend on length. Visual quality barely predicts engagement ([Li 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.00289)).
  - TikToks of 5–10 s have a median watch time of 3.1 s ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/)). The average Reel watch time is 8.5 s (Metricool, 24.4M posts; [NetInfluencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/instagram-views-up-27-percent-in-early-2026-as-publishing-increases-report-finds/)).
- **Organic length.** [M]
  - TikTok brand videos (6M, first half of 2026): engagement rate peaks at 15–30 s (6.0%); median views peak at 120–180 s ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-are-tiktok-videos/)).
  - TikToks over 60 s: +43.2% reach and +63.8% watch time compared with 30–60 s (1.1M videos; Buffer).
  - Reels: 45–60 s performs best. Reels over 3 min are not recommended to non-followers ([Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)).
- **Ad length.** [M/P]
  - Best CTR: 15–30 s on Meta (1.72%), 9–15 s on TikTok (0.96%), 6–15 s on Shorts (1.14%). CTR falls past about 60 s.
  - Completion for ads under 15 s: 32% on Meta, 42% on TikTok (AdBeacon/Benly).
  - Google: Shorts ads under 60 s, because only the first 60 s play; 10–30 s for action campaigns ([Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697)).
- **Cut pace.** In 2,511 TikTok seller videos, shot count had an inverted-U effect on engagement: too few and too many cuts both hurt. Faster speech raised shares ([Xiao, Li & Mou 2024](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/INTR-07-2023-0521/full/html)). TikTok says faster scene changes pull viewers in early (Creative Codes). "Cut every 2–3 s" is creator lore. [S; A]
- **Pace and readability.** [S; A]
  - Subtitles are readable at 160–180 wpm; above 200 wpm is hard to follow ([BBC R&D](https://bbc.co.uk/rd/blog/2015-09-how-fast-should-subtitles-be)).
  - Netflix limits: at most 20 characters per second, 42 characters per line and 2 lines, with each subtitle on screen 0.83–7 s ([Netflix](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-USA-Timed-Text-Style-Guide); [general](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements)).
  - TikTok's "5–10 words per second" text guidance conflicts with this reading research; treat it as a ceiling, not a target.
- **Sound.** 88% of TikTok users say sound is vital (Creative Codes), and music or voice-over lifts Shorts ad conversions by more than 20% (Google). [S] A dated 2019 survey (69% watch muted in public; 80% more likely to finish with captions) still supports burned-in captions ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tjmccue/2019/07/31/verizon-media-says-69-percent-of-consumers-watching-video-with-sound-off/)). [M]
- **Safe zones on a 1080×1920 frame (derived from official templates).** Margins to keep clear:

  | Platform | Top | Bottom | Left | Right |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | TikTok | 240 px | 660 px | 120 px | 120 px, plus a button column at x>780 below y≈840 |
  | Reels | 269 px (14%) | 672 px (35%); 768 px (40%) with a disclaimer | 65 px (6%) | 65 px (6%) |
  | Shorts | 288 px | 672 px | 48 px | 192 px |

  A box safe on all three is x 120–888, y 288–1248 ([Upload-Post, Sep 2026](https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/)). Meta found ads that respect the safe zone get +39% CTR (playbook). Meta unified its Stories and Reels safe zone in March 2026; keep content in the central ~80% of the width ([Billo](https://billo.app/blog/meta-ads-safe-zones/)). [P/S/M]

## 3. Platform algorithm and policy updates, 2025–2026

**TikTok**
- **US joint venture.** It closed on 22 Jan 2026; the US recommender is being retrained on US data ([MTSU](https://firstamendment.mtsu.edu/post/tiktok-finalizes-deal-to-form-new-american-entity/)). Re-baseline benchmarks. [M]
- **Search.** 57% of users search, and 25% do so within 30 s of opening the app ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-GB/blog/introducing-search-ads-campaign)). Put keywords in the spoken line, the on-screen text and the caption. [S]
- **AI-generated content (AIGC).** Realistic AIGC must be labelled. TikTok reads C2PA content credentials and has labelled 1.3B+ videos. Since Nov 2025 it is testing invisible watermarks and a slider that lets users see less AIGC ([TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-spot-shape-and-understand-ai-content)). [P]
- **Ad policy.** Undisclosed AIGC gets the ad rejected, and AI voice cloning counts as a significant edit. Absolute claims such as "#1" and misleading before/after shots are banned ([TikTok ad policy](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content)). [P]
- **TikTok Shop 2026 (reported by third parties).** [P]
  - Content Policy (22 May) and Listing Policy (2 Jun): ban misleading or "lowest price" claims, false coupons, unsupported transformation claims and selling on sympathy. Daily limits on shoppable posts began 11 May ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-tightening-grip-new-rules-that-could-freeze-your-account/)).
  - If 5 or more shoppable videos in 7 days are "non-interactive" (static images with music, looping clips) or misleading, you are capped at 7 product-linked videos for the next 7 days ([BigSeller](https://www.bigseller.com/blog/articleDetails/tiktok-shop-content-posting-limit-update.4377.htm)).
  - Quality rules (23 May): at least 3 s of dynamic footage, a real-world setting, visible people, a multi-angle demo, text in sync with speech, and TikTok-library music only. Product-page screenshots are banned. AI voices are banned in LIVE ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-quality-rules-ban-ai-voices-and-still-images-from-lives/)).

**Instagram**
- **Ranking signals.** Head of Instagram Adam Mosseri said in Jan 2025 that the top three signals are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)). [S]
- **Originality.** [P]
  - Accounts that repost others' content 10 or more times in 30 days lose recommendation eligibility.
  - Watermarked content, clickbait and engagement bait are ineligible ([Instagram](https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-originality?locale=en_US)).
  - This was extended to photos and carousels on 30 Apr 2026 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/)).
  - On Facebook, Meta demotes posts that explicitly ask for comments, likes, shares or tags ([Meta](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait/)).
- **Trial Reels.** These are shown to non-followers first; Instagram reports +80% non-follower Reels reach ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-allows-creators-to-schedule-trial-reels/816549/)). [S, self-reported]
- **Length and hashtags.** Reels over 3 min are not recommended to non-followers. Hashtags are capped at 5 per post since Dec 2025 ([SMT](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)). [P]
- **Authenticity.** Mosseri's 31 Dec 2025 memo says it will be more practical to fingerprint real media than to detect fakes ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-chief-ai-is-so-ubiquitous-it-will-be-more-practical-to-fingerprint-real-media-than-fake-media-202620080.html)). [S]

**YouTube Shorts**
- **Length and music.** Shorts can run up to 3 min. From 24 Sep 2026, Shorts of 1–3 min with a Content ID claim are no longer auto-blocked. Most library songs can be used for up to 90 s ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en)). [P]
- **View counting.** A view now counts on any start or replay: for Shorts since 31 Mar 2025 ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en-GB)), for all formats from 24 Aug 2026. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) pay still uses "engaged views" ([Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2991785?hl=en-EN)). [P]
- **AI labels (27 May 2026).** Shorts get an on-video overlay label. YouTube auto-labels content where it detects photorealistic AI. The label does not affect reach or monetization ([Google](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/improving-ai-labels-for-viewers-and-creators/)). [P]
- **Other rules.** YPP's "inauthentic content" policy, clarified on 15 Jul 2025, excludes mass-produced or repetitive videos from monetization ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en&p=reused_content)). With more than 60 hashtags, all are ignored ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)). [P]

## 4. Ad-creative guidance from the platforms

- **Google ABCD (Attention, Branding, Connection, Direction).** [S/P]
  - Ads that follow it show a 30% lift in short-term sales likelihood and a 17% lift in long-term brand contribution.
  - For Shorts, Google leads with Connection: show the talent using the product. Then Attention: announce the ad with audio and use tight framing. Then organic Branding, then a clear CTA. For action ads, make the product the ad and ask only after the context is set ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en)).
  - The Shorts CTA button appears at 3 s (Performance Max and Demand Gen) or at 10 s (video view and reach campaigns). Use a 40-character headline and a 90-character description ([Google](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697)).
- **TikTok.** [S]
  - Structure: hook, then selling points, then CTA. Use 3–5 creatives per ad group, with variants that differ substantially.
  - CTA cards: +45% recall and +19% likeability.
  - Footage at 720p or higher: +312% conversions ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-that-drives-conversions)).
- **Meta.** [M]
  - Agencies briefed by Meta report that its system treats ads with the same look or the same opening hook as one ad, so text-only swaps do not count as new creatives ([Marketing Brew, Sep 2026](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/meta-ad-creative-diversification-best-practices-advertiser-approach)).
  - Meta claims $4.13 returned per $1 of ad spend in its own study. Cannes 2026 added brand memory and voice-over translation into 11+ languages ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-ad-spend-returns-4-13-per-dollar-as-ai-creative-tools-roll-out-at-cannes/)).
- **Winners are rare.** Only about 5% of creatives reach 10× their account's median spend ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/research/creative-benchmarks-2026/)). Produce batches rather than polishing one ad. [M]

## 5. AI UGC and script tools: features worth copying

- **From product input to variants.**
  - Creatify: URL-to-video, with Batch Mode producing 10+ variants ([Creatify](https://creatify.ai/features/batch-mode)).
  - Arcads: script to AI-actor video in about 2 min, with hook, angle and actor variants. Actors are cloned from consenting performers ([Datastudios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/arcads-ai-ugc-video-ad-generation-credit-costs-and-actor-realism-explained)).
  - TikTok Symphony Agent: brief → insight report on top ads' hooks and CTAs → storyboard → video, with up to 3 variants per run ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/symphony-agent)).
- **Scoring.**
  - OpusClip: a 0–99 score built from Hook, Flow, Value and Trend ([Opus](https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/virality-score)). It works as a sorting tool, not a forecast ([review](https://autoposting.ai/blog/opus-clip-review)). [A]
  - Pencil: a 0–100 predicted-performance score, plus an LLM "share of model" brand score ([Pencil](https://trypencil.com/blog/articles/pencil-media-performance-score)).
  - Motion: 0–100 Hook, Watch, Click and Convert scores, plus tactic tagging ([Motion](https://motionapp.com/library/talk/tell-stories-and-build-hypotheses-with-metrics/)).
- **Localization and likeness.**
  - HeyGen: 175+ languages with lip-sync ([HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com/tool/ugc-ads-maker)).
  - Captions (Mirage): an audio-driven actor model ([Captions](https://captions.ai/help/mirage/mirage)).
  - Synthesia: its terms **forbid stock avatars in paid or boosted social UGC** ([Synthesia](https://help.synthesia.io/en/articles/8330564-platform-integrity)).
  - TikTok Symphony: stock avatars are paid actors licensed for commercial use ([TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-gb/symphony-avatars)).
  - MakeUGC: product-in-hand clips, reportedly capped at 10 s ([review](https://tagshop.ai/review/makeugc-ai/)). [A]
- **Templates.** Jasper's explainer template runs problem → solution → how it works → CTA ([Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/blog/video-script-templates)).
- **What to build:** 3+ visually distinct hook variants, readable sub-scores, brand kits, a disclosure pack per market, and tactic/format tags for learning from results.

## 6. Disclosure and compliance: what each script must include

- **Paid, gifted or affiliate content.** [P]
  - Disclose inside the video itself. Captions alone, end cards and hashtag piles are not enough.
  - If claims are both shown and spoken, the disclosure should be both visible and spoken, in the same language.
  - A platform toggle alone may be insufficient ([16 CFR 255.5](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-255); [FTC Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)).
  - Still turn on TikTok's commercial content toggle ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/global/bc-policy/en)) and YouTube's paid-promotion toggle ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235)).
  - France requires "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale", and "Image virtuelle" for AI-generated images ([Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/jorf/article_jo/JORFARTI000047663211); [MDC Avocat](https://mdc-avocat.fr/quelles-sont-les-mentions-obligatoires-sur-les-publications-des-influenceurs/)).
- **AI personas cannot give testimonials.** The FTC's rule bans testimonials that misrepresent that the person exists or used the product. Penalties are up to $53,088 per violation ([16 CFR 465.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-465); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/12/ftc-warns-10-companies-about-possible-violations-agencys-new-consumer-review-rule)). [P]
- **Synthetic-presenter labels.** [P]
  - **New York (from 9 Jun 2026):** a conspicuous disclosure is required whenever an ad shows an AI-generated human. Penalties are $1k for a first violation and $5k after that ([Crowell](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/synthetic-performers-real-consequences-implications-of-trailblazing-new-york-ai-ad-law)).
  - **EU AI Act Article 50 (applies from 2 Aug 2026):** realistic AI video needs a clearly visible label plus a machine-readable mark. The EU provides an icon set, and fines go up to €15M or 3% of turnover ([Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-transparent-ai-2026-08-02_en); [Goodwin](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force)). According to a law-firm reading of the guidance, a plausible but fictional person is in scope ([GT Law](https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/6/deepfakes-chatbots-ai-generated-text-european-commission-details-transparency-obligations-under-the-ai-act)).
  - **China (from 1 Sep 2025):** a visible label on the video's opening screen plus a metadata label; do not strip either ([CLT](https://www.chinalawtranslate.com/en/ai-labeling/)).
  - **India (from 20 Feb 2026):** photorealistic synthetic content needs a prominent label ([The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/govt-tightens-rules-on-ai-generated-deepfake-content-mandates-faster-takedown-of-flagged-material/article70615674.ece)).
  - **Platforms:** TikTok AIGC label ([TikTok](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)). YouTube altered/synthetic toggle ([YouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). Meta "AI info" labels ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/)), with self-disclosure required for photorealistic video ([PCMag](https://www.pcmag.com/news/meta-will-label-ai-generated-content-but-theres-a-catch)).
- **Disclosure block every script should carry** (derived from the rules above):
  1. On screen: "Ad · AI-generated presenter" from 0 s for at least 3 s, at least caption size, inside the safe zone.
  2. Spoken "ad" or "I earn a commission" within 5 s.
  3. The first caption line repeats both.
  4. Platform toggles switched on.
  5. The render brief asks for C2PA, keeps metadata, and adds the EU icon for the EU and an opening-screen label for China.
  6. No claims of personal experience from AI presenters.

## 7. Academic work on popularity prediction

- **What wins prediction challenges.** The SMP 2025 video-track winner used pretrained video features, user metadata and gradient boosting ([MVP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.00950)). At VQualA/ICCV 2025 (97 participants), large multimodal models won, and the audio-aware model beat the vision-only one ([Sun et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02516)). Creator and context features dominate; audio matters. [S]
- **The ceiling for script-only scoring.** An MLLM annotated 77 theory-based variables on about 10k brand TikToks. It gave only modest gains over account-size and video-age baselines, and most variables showed no effect ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16053)). [S]
- **Faces beat text.** Across 163k TikToks, facial expressions predicted views better than text sentiment ([2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15278)). [M]
- **Retrieval helps.** Scoring against a memory bank of similar videos improves popularity prediction ([3M, 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.17038)). [M]
- **Shot scale and audio.** Close-up and medium shots were predictive in 400 TikToks ([2021](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/355925066_Slapping_Cats_Bopping_Heads_and_Oreo_Shakes_Understanding_Indicators_of_Virality_in_TikTok_Short_Videos)). Brighter audio (higher spectral centroid) reduced likes (Xiao 2024). [M/S]

## Cross-check against the current RULES.md (read-only)

- "85% watch sound-off" is an old Facebook figure. Current evidence points to sound-on viewing, so design for sound but keep videos readable when muted.
- A verbal hook of "≤8 words in 0.5–1.5 s" can't be spoken naturally: 8 words at 3 words per second take about 2.7 s. Start by 0.3 s and finish by 3.0 s.
- The "quiet" AI disclosure style must still be conspicuous (New York) and clearly visible (EU).
- A 28-word verdict card (about 150 characters) needs at least 7.5 s at 20 characters per second, over the 7 s per-card maximum. Split it (relevant to open item D103).
- An AI presenter on TikTok Shop conflicts with the Shop quality rules. Route those scripts to a human or a hybrid.
- The source of "+26% clicks with a caption" was not found.
